CHIPLUN EDUCATION SOCIETY — INSTITUTIONAL TIMELINE

From Two Classrooms in 1970 to a Full Campus Cluster

The Chiplun Education Society manages a growing cluster of institutions, each launched in response to the community's evolving educational needs along the Konkan coastline.

1961 — SOCIETY ESTABLISHED

Chiplun Education Society

Founded by philanthropists from Dadar Mohalla, Desai Mohalla, Bahadur Sheikh, Jam-e-Masjid Mohalla, Muradpur, and Mithagari Mohalla with a shared conviction: that social progress was inseparable from education.
1970 — FLAGSHIP SCHOOL FOUNDED

Maharashtra High School

Began operations in Muradpur, Dadar Mohalla (Chiplun 415605) with two classrooms. Grew steadily to serve the Upper Primary and Secondary divisions under the Urdu medium and Maharashtra State Board.
1981 — PRIMARY DIVISION EXPANSION

M.I. Wangde Nursery & Haji Shahabuddin Wangde Primary School

Two sister institutions launched to provide a full pre-primary and primary feeder pipeline into the high school, completing the K–12 pathway for Muradpur families.
2000 — HIGHER SECONDARY LAUNCHED

Haji A.E. Kalsekar Junior College of Arts & Commerce

Established on the Maharashtra High School campus to accommodate the growing demand for post-SSC education. Offers Arts and Commerce streams under the Maharashtra HSC Board (Konkan Division).
2021 — COMMUNITY RESILIENCE

Flood Recovery & Alumni Aid

During the devastating July 2021 Chiplun floods (water levels exceeding 20 feet in some areas), the school building was used as a relief shelter. Alumni launched financial recovery appeals that helped rebuild facilities and restore the campus library.
TODAY — ACTIVE & EXPANDING

Urdu Medium, Mid-Day Meals & Computer Lab

A private-aided co-educational institution serving Std V to XII. Features include on-site mid-day meals, 3,400+ volume library, computer lab, and playground in the Muradpur locality, 250 km south of Mumbai on NH-66.
