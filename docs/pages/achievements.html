SSC BOARD RESULTS — MSBSHSE PUNE DIVISION

Academic Excellence Since 1970

Since its founding in 1970, Maharashtra High School has maintained a sustained record of academic achievement in the Maharashtra SSC Board examinations (Pune Division). The school's Urdu medium instruction — combined with Semi-English Science tracks — has produced graduates who have gone on to careers in medicine, engineering, civil services, and commerce across Maharashtra and beyond.

INSTITUTIONAL & COMMUNITY RECOGNITION

Beyond the Classroom

2021 — FLOOD RELIEF SERVICE

Community Emergency Shelter

During the July 2021 Chiplun floods (among the worst in the city's history, with water levels exceeding 20 feet in parts of Muradpur), the school campus was repurposed as a government-designated emergency relief and temporary shelter. Staff and alumni cleared sludge and restored the 3,400+ volume library through community fundraising.
INTER-SCHOOL COMPETITIONS

Ratnagiri District Debate & Quiz Championships

School debate and quiz teams have consistently reached district-level finals in the Ratnagiri District inter-school competitions. The Urdu elocution team placed second in the Konkan Division competition in 2024, coached by Senior Urdu Master Tariq M. Khan.
NATIONAL PROGRAMME PARTICIPATION

Mid-Day Meals & National Scholarship Schemes

The school participates in the National Mid-Day Meal Scheme (Std I–VIII), the Pre-Matric Scholarship for Minorities (Government of India, Ministry of Minority Affairs), and the Maulana Azad National Fellowship — with eligible students guided through applications each year.
