CURRICULUM & TIMETABLES — MSBSHSE PUNE DIVISION

Academic Framework, Syllabus & Calendar 2026–27

Maharashtra High School follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE), Pune Division. All textbooks are sourced from Balbharati (Maharashtra State Bureau of Textbook Production and Curriculum Research). Digital versions are available at ebalbharati.in.

Note: SSC Board 2026 examinations have concluded (Theory: Feb 20–Mar 18, 2026; Practical & Viva: Feb 2–18, 2026). The 2026–27 timetable will be published at mahahsscboard.in by October 2026.

UPPER PRIMARY DIVISION — STD V TO VII (URDU MEDIUM)

Evaluation system: Continuous Comprehensive Evaluation (CCE). No single high-stakes examination — assessment is continuous through formative and summative components throughout the year.

HIGHER SECONDARY — STD XI–XII — HAJI A.E. KALSEKAR JUNIOR COLLEGE (ARTS & COMMERCE)
