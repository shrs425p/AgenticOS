HOW TO REACH — CHIPLUN, RATNAGIRI

Getting to Muradpur Campus

MODE OF TRAVEL ORIGIN DETAILS APPROX. TIME
Train (Konkan Railway) Mumbai Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT) Direct trains to Chiplun Station (Mumbai–Mangaluru corridor, NH-66 parallel). Alight at Chiplun Railway Station. School is ~10 min walk or auto from station through Muradpur locality. ~4–5 hours from Mumbai
Road (NH-66) Mumbai (via Mumbai–Goa Highway / NH-66) Chiplun is ~250 km from Mumbai. Enter city via NH-66, follow road to Muradpur locality, Dadar Mohalla. GPS: 17.537964°N, 73.516538°E. ~5–6 hours by road
Road (from Ratnagiri City) Ratnagiri City HQ Chiplun is ~90 km north of Ratnagiri on NH-66. State transport (MSRTC) buses available. ~1.5–2 hours
Local Auto-Rickshaw Chiplun Bus Stand or Railway Station Ask for "Maharashtra High School, Muradpur" or "Dadar Mohalla School". Auto-rickshaws are readily available from both the bus stand and railway station. ~5–10 minutes
