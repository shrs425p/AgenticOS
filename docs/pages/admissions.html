HOW TO JOIN — 4 SIMPLE STEPS

Step-by-Step Admission Process

Follow these steps to enrol your child for the 2026–27 academic session. Our school administrative office is open to assist parents with all paperwork.

01

Collect Application Form

Obtain the admission registration form directly from the School Office in Muradpur, Chiplun (07:30 AM – 05:30 PM), or submit an online inquiry below to pre-register.

02

Submit Required Documents

Submit the filled form along with the student's previous school Leaving Certificate (LC / TC), Progress Report Card, Aadhaar Card copy, and passport photographs.

03

Verification & Enrolment

The Headmaster and Admission Committee verify documents. Enrolment is confirmed on the official General Register (G.R.) and student UDISE/SARAL portal.

04

Class Allotment & Kit

Receive student ID, roll number, division allotment, academic timetable, textbook guidance, and uniform details ready for the first day of school.

PARENT ASSISTANCE

Frequently Asked Questions on Admissions

What is the medium of instruction at Maharashtra High School?
The primary medium of instruction is Urdu. Mathematics and Science subjects are taught in Semi-English to build strong scientific and technical terminology for students preparing for higher education.
Can students from other primary schools take admission in Standard V?
Yes. Students completing Standard IV from any recognized Municipal, Zilla Parishad, or Private Primary School are eligible for direct admission to Standard V upon submitting their School Leaving Certificate (T.C.).
What streams are offered at the Junior College (Std XI & XII)?
Haji A.E. Kalsekar Junior College on the same campus offers both Arts and Commerce streams affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board (Konkan Division).
Are there any tuition fees charged during the academic year?
No. As a government grant-in-aid institution, tuition is 100% free. Students only pay minor government-prescribed term activity and exam registration charges.
