HOW TO FILE AN RTI APPLICATION — STEP BY STEP

STEP 1
Draft your application

Limit to a single subject. Maximum 150 words. Include your name, address, and a declaration of Indian citizenship.

STEP 2
Attach Photo ID

Self-attested copy of Aadhaar or Voter ID. BPL card holders must attach their card to claim fee exemption.

STEP 3
Pay ₹30 Fee

Online via aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in, or cash / court fee stamps / IPO / Demand Draft to PIO directly. BPL applicants: exempt.

STEP 4
Submit to PIO

Address to the school Principal (PIO). Submit online at rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in, or post / hand-deliver to campus office.

MAHARASHTRA RTI RULES 2026 — FULL PROCEDURAL MANDATE SUMMARY [ + ]
  • 150-Word Application Constraint: Applications must be concise and cover a single subject matter. Multi-subject queries may result in only the first issue being processed.
  • Mandatory Photo ID: Self-attested proof of Indian citizenship (Aadhaar Card or Voter ID) must accompany every application.
  • Application Fee: ₹30 — payable by cash, court fee stamp, IPO, demand draft, or online at aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in.
  • Document Copying: ₹5 per page. First hour of record inspection: free. Subsequent hours: ₹50/hour.
  • PIO Response Timeline: 30 calendar days from receipt of application (15 days if the matter concerns the life or liberty of a person).
  • Citizen Declaration: Each application must include a statement that the applicant is an Indian citizen.
BPL FEE EXEMPTION — BELOW POVERTY LINE APPLICANTS [ + ]

Citizens holding a valid Below Poverty Line (BPL) ration card issued by the Maharashtra State Food & Civil Supplies Department are completely exempt from the ₹30 application fee. BPL applicants are entitled to the first 50 pages of requested information free of cost. Pages beyond 50 are charged at the standard ₹5/page rate. The BPL ration card must be submitted as a self-attested photocopy along with the RTI application.

FIRST APPEAL — SECTION 19(1) RTI ACT 2005 [ + ]

If the PIO does not respond within 30 calendar days, or if you are dissatisfied with the information provided, you may file a First Appeal under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act 2005 with the designated First Appellate Authority (FAA) — the Chiplun Education Society Management Committee Chairman. The first appeal must be filed within 30 days of the PIO's decision (or non-decision). First Appeal Fee: ₹50. The FAA must dispose of the appeal within 30 days (extendable to 45 days with written reasons).

SECOND APPEAL — STATE INFORMATION COMMISSION (SIC) [ + ]

If the First Appeal is dismissed or unsatisfactory, a Second Appeal may be preferred to the Maharashtra State Information Commission (SIC). Second Appeal Fee: ₹100. The SIC may impose penalties on the PIO for unjustified withholding of information. Online filing available at rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in.

DISTRICT EDUCATION OVERSIGHT — ZILLA PARISHAD, RATNAGIRI [ + ]

Secondary education governance for Maharashtra High School is monitored by the Education Department (Secondary), Zilla Parishad, Ratnagiri. The DEO Ratnagiri office is the intermediary between the school and the State Education Department for matters including UDISE reporting, grant-in-aid disbursement, fee regulation, and RTE seat allocation. Contact: (02352) 222101. Appeals regarding district education policy may be submitted via rtionline.maharashtra.gov.in.

SECTION 4(1)(B) PROACTIVE DISCLOSURES — WHAT THIS SCHOOL PUBLISHES [ + ]

Under Section 4(1)(b) of the RTI Act, the following information is proactively published and available at the school office (Mon–Sat, 09:00–13:00):

  • Organisation structure, functions, and duties of all staff
  • Powers and duties of officers and employees
  • Decision-making process and supervision channels
  • Standards set for discharging functions (academic performance benchmarks)
  • Rules, regulations, and documents held by the school
  • FRC-approved fee schedule (see Admissions page)
  • Annual UDISE data returns submitted to the State Government
  • RTE 25% seat occupancy data per academic year
  • Grant-in-Aid accounts (available for inspection on prior appointment)
